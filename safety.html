Child Safety Standards for Soko

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Our Commitment

Soko has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). We prohibit any content or behaviour that sexually exploits, endangers, or harms minors. Violations result in immediate account termination and, where appropriate, reporting to Kenyan authorities and relevant international bodies.

Age Restriction

Soko is for adults aged 18 and over. Users confirm their date of birth during signup, and the app enforces this restriction. Accounts belonging to minors are removed without notice.

In-App Reporting

Every user can report or block any other user directly from their profile or from a chat. Reports are reviewed within 48 hours. Users can also report concerns by email at any time.

Prevention Measures

• Age verification enforced at signup and in the database

• Report and block features available on every profile and chat

• Reported accounts are reviewed and may be suspended or removed

• No tolerance for accounts that violate these standards

Dedicated Contact

For child safety concerns, contact us at: sokodating@gmail.com

This inbox is monitored regularly, and child safety reports are prioritised.

Compliance

Soko complies with all applicable child safety laws and regulations, including Google Play's Child Safety Standards policy and Kenyan law.
