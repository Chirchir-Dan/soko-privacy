Child Safety Standards for Soko

Last updated: August 2026

Our Commitment

Soko is committed to protecting children from sexual abuse and exploitation. We have zero tolerance for any content or behavior that exploits or endangers children.

Age Restrictions

Users must be 18 years or older to use Soko. We verify age through date of birth during profile setup.

Reporting

To report child safety concerns, contact us at: sokosupport@gmail.com

Prevention Measures

• Age verification at signup

• Profile photos are reviewed

• Users can be reported and blocked

Compliance

Soko complies with all applicable child safety laws and regulations.
